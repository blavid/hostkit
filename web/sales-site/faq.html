Getting started

What do I need?

A Roku device on the TV at your rental and a Wi-Fi connection. Guests use the Roku remote they already have. They don’t need an app or an account.

How do I connect my TV?

Add the HostKit channel on the Roku and open it. The TV shows a 6-character code and a QR code. Scan the QR with your phone and the code is filled in for you. Codes refresh every 15 minutes, so if one expires, use the new one on screen.

Can I bring my listing over from Airbnb or Vrbo?

Yes. Paste your listing text or use an official export, and HostKit drafts your property description and starter FAQs from it. You review every sentence before anything is saved.

Do I need Host Tools?

No. Connecting a Host Tools account is optional. With it, each guest’s name and stay dates come from your reservations. Without it, you add them for each stay in the setup wizard.

Personalization

Where does the guest’s name come from?

From your reservations: connect Host Tools, or type the guest name and dates for each stay in the setup wizard. The TV shows the name only during that stay.

Is the guest’s name kept private?

Yes. Guest names are private details, like your Wi-Fi password. They’re only sent to the TVs you’ve paired.

Where does the forecast come from?

The welcome screen shows the local forecast for your property’s location during each stay.

What do “picked for your stay” and the daily tip use?

Your own list of things to do and tips. HostKit orders the picks for the guest’s dates and the forecast, and shows a different tip each day.

Pricing and billing

What does it cost?

30-day free trial, then $5/mo per property.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No credit card required. Start your trial with just an account.

What if a payment fails during a stay?

There’s a 14-day grace period. HostKit won’t switch a guest’s TV off in the middle of their stay.

Privacy and reliability

Is my Wi-Fi password safe?

Private details like your Wi-Fi password go in a separate private panel. They’re only sent to TVs you’ve paired.

What if the internet drops?

The TV keeps showing the last guide it downloaded, so guests aren’t left with a blank screen.

Updates and TVs

How do updates reach the TV?

Edit your guide in the setup wizard and publish. The TV picks up changes when it starts and about every 6 hours.

Can I remove a TV?

Yes. You can remove a paired TV from your account at any time, and it goes back to the setup screen.
